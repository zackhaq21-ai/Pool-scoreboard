● TONIGHT'S CLUB

ROSTER

Teams and players on this offline board. Add players at the top, or use the big ✕ Remove button on any player's row to take them off. Sit someone out and they leave the rotation until they're back in.

＋ Add a player

Type a name and tap Add — or just press Enter. They join the roster, 1v1 Solo and Cutthroat right away. Adding a name you removed before brings that player back with their records.

Removing a player

Removing takes a player off the roster, out of the Solo / Cutthroat / King of the Hill rotation, and off their team — the team's record stays. It does not erase scores or game history: their name stays in past games, and you can undo it, or add the exact same name back to restore them.

Offline & your data

This copy runs entirely on this device — no internet needed after you open it. Every win you record is saved in this browser automatically. It does not update your main board by itself.

After the night: export a backup here, then give that file to Muse and the offline games will be brought into your main Pool Night Scoreboard.

Restore replaces this offline board's state with the backup's. Your main board is never touched from here.
